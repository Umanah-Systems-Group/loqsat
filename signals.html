Share of standing cane cut so far, 2024 season.

What orbit sees.

Standing cane is dark green. A cut field turns pale almost overnight. Week by week, the pale blocks spread across the region, and we count them.

Why sugar cane first
Brazil’s Centre-South is the world’s largest sugar cane region. The pace of the harvest moves sugar and ethanol prices, and the official crush reports arrive only twice a month, after the fields are cut.
Who it is for
Commodity funds, trading desks and agribusiness teams that want the harvest pace before the official numbers.
